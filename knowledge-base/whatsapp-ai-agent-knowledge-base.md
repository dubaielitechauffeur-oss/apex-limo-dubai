#### Q: What is included in a luxury chauffeur service in Dubai?

#### Q: How far in advance should I book a luxury chauffeur in Dubai?
A: Same-day and next-day bookings are usually possible, but booking 24–48 hours ahead guarantees your preferred vehicle, and further ahead for weddings or larger groups.

#### Q: Is a chauffeur airport transfer more expensive than a taxi in Dubai?
A: It's typically priced higher than a base metered taxi fare, but the price is fixed regardless of delays or traffic, while a taxi meter can climb well past an initial estimate.

#### Q: What happens if my flight is delayed and I've booked a chauffeur?
A: A properly run transfer tracks your flight automatically and adjusts your pickup time at no extra charge — you won't be billed for an airline delay.

#### Q: Do chauffeur services cover both DXB and DWC?

#### Q: Can I book a chauffeur transfer for a same-day arrival?
A: Often yes, though booking 24–48 hours ahead is recommended to guarantee your preferred vehicle rather than a same-category substitute.

#### Q: Is the Mercedes S-Class good for airport transfers in Dubai?
A: Yes — it's the most-booked vehicle for DXB and DWC transfers, combining quick loading with the quietest cabin in a typical executive fleet.

#### Q: How many passengers and bags fit in an S-Class?
A: Comfortably three passengers with two standard suitcases plus carry-ons — for larger groups or more luggage, a V-Class or Escalade is a better fit.

A: Yes, hourly and full-day hire are both available, with the same chauffeur and vehicle staying with you between stops.

#### Q: Can a corporate chauffeur account be set up before our first visit?

#### Q: Can chauffeurs sign NDAs for sensitive corporate visits?
A: Yes, most established chauffeur services can accommodate confidentiality agreements for high-sensitivity bookings, particularly for delegations or M&A-related travel.

#### Q: Is it normal to request the same driver for a multi-day visit?
A: Yes, and it's worth explicitly requesting — a consistent driver across a visit builds familiarity with your schedule that a rotating pool can't match.

A: With an established account, same-day additions are typically confirmed within minutes rather than requiring a fresh booking process each time.

#### Q: How long does a typical Dubai city tour take?

#### Q: Can the chauffeur suggest an itinerary if I'm not sure what to see?
A: Yes — experienced chauffeurs can recommend a route based on your interests, the time of day, and how much time you have.

#### Q: Is a private city tour suitable for families with young children?

#### Q: Does the car wait for me at each stop during a city tour?

#### Q: How far in advance should we book a wedding chauffeur in Dubai?

#### Q: Can you coordinate transport for the whole wedding party, not just the couple?
